Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

What GameShelf doesn't do

GameShelf doesn't require creating an account, doesn't collect any personal data, and doesn't integrate any analytics, ad-tracking, or audience-measurement tool.

Data stored on your device

Your game collection (statuses, dates added) and your preferences (e.g. daily reminder time) are stored only on your device via Apple SwiftData. They are never transmitted to a server controlled by GameShelf — no such server exists.

Third-party services used

GameShelf queries the IGDB API (owned by Twitch Interactive / Amazon) to fetch public video game information (titles, cover art, platforms, release dates). These requests identify the app to IGDB, not you personally. See IGDB/Twitch's own privacy policy for details on how they handle these standard network requests.

In-app purchases

If a "GameShelf Pro" purchase is offered, it is handled entirely by Apple via StoreKit. GameShelf never sees or stores any payment information — refer to Apple's own privacy policy for how purchases are handled.

Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device via Apple UserNotifications — no data is sent to a server to trigger this notification.
